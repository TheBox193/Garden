**PUMPKIN PIE**

Make Pastry for One-Crust Pie. Line pie pan. Build up high fluted edge.

**For the Filling**

For 9" Pie:

- 1¾ cups mashed cooked pumpkin
- ½ tsp. salt
- 1⅓ cups milk
- 2 large eggs (½ cup) or 4 yolks
- ⅔ cup brown sugar
- 2 tbsp. sugar
- 1¼ tsp. cinnamon
- ½ tsp. ginger
- ½ tsp. nutmeg
- ¼ tsp. cloves

For 8" Pie:

- 1¼ cups mashed cooked pumpkin
- ⅓ tsp. salt
- 1 cups milk
- 2 eggs (⅓ cup) or 3 yolks
- ½ cup brown sugar
- 1½ tbsp. sugar
- 1 tsp. cinnamon
- ⅓ tsp. ginger
- ⅓ tsp. nutmeg
- ¼ tsp. cloves

Beat together with a rotary beater...

Pour into... fine strainer. Pour into lined pie pan. Bake just until a knife inserted into the side of filling comes out clean. The center may still look soft but will set later. Serve slightly warm or cold.

**NOTE:** This is a rich brown spicy pie. For a lighter color and milder flavor, use all white sugar and omit cloves.

**TEMPERATURE:** 425° (hot oven).

**TIME:** Bake 45 to 55 min.
